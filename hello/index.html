Web engineering, cloud infrastructure and security, and engineering mentoring. Code: Python, Go, TypeScript, Ruby, PHP. Infrastructure: AWS, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD. I work remotely; email me and we can talk through what you need.

Yes. For small sites I usually recommend static pages or WordPress on pages.alldaycity.com, with a walkthrough so you can manage it yourself afterwards.
