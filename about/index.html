Who we are

A small software engineering and cloud consultancy operating between London and Southeast Asia (Malaysia / Singapore). Started in web development around 2000 — we’ve been building and shipping things ever since.

We keep teams tight: at most three or four people on any engagement. You deal directly with the people doing the work.

What we believe

Build for the long run.

Our approach is simple, clear technology. Straightforward pricing, mature open-source foundations, and solutions designed to run without drama. We’d rather hand over something you can maintain and understand than something impressive that breaks quietly.

Common questions

What kind of work do you take on?

Web engineering and cloud infrastructure. On the code side: Python, Go, TypeScript, Ruby, PHP — we’ve worked across stacks on projects of various sizes. On the infrastructure side: AWS, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD pipelines. We work remotely. Drop us an email and we can talk through what you need.

I just need a simple website — can you help?

Yes. For small sites we usually recommend static pages or WordPress hosted on pages.alldaycity.com. We include a walkthrough so you can manage it yourself going forward.

How does pricing work?

Fixed quotes where possible, hourly where the scope is genuinely uncertain. We aim for no surprises — if something changes mid-project, we’ll tell you before it affects the cost.
